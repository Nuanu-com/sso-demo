No SSO client configured

SSO_CLIENT_ID and SSO_CLIENT_SECRET are empty. An SSO superuser registers a client for you; the redirect URI below has to be in its allowed_redirect_urls, matched as an exact string. 

curl -X POST "{{ .Provider.BaseURL }}/api/v1/oauth_clients/" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <superuser_access_token>" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"client_name": "SSO Demo",
       "allowed_redirect_urls": "{{ .Provider.RedirectURI }},{{ .Provider.PostLogoutURI }}"}'

Put the returned id and secret in your .env.local and restart.
